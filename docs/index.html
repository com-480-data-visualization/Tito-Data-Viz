We asked the question.

Does EA's Overall rating match how players actually perform? A single number, 50 to 99, speaks for every footballer in the game.

02

The data answered.

We built a composite from 11 to 18 real match stats tailored to each position. Each composite sits next to the EA OVR for the same player. The distance between the two is the Reputation Gap: who is overrated, who is underrated.
